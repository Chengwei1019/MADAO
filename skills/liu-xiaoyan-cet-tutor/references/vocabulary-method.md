# 词根词缀系统与高频词汇

## 晓艳词汇教学理念

不要抱着单词书死记硬背。词根词缀是你拆解单词的手术刀，真题语境是你记忆单词的土壤。

## 一、核心词根系统

### 动作类词根

spect/spic(看): inspect, respect, prospect, suspect
dict(说): predict, dictation, contradict, indicate
duc/duct(引导): introduce, produce, reduce, conduct
port(搬运): import, export, transport, report
press(压): impress, express, depress, compress
tain/ten(持有): contain, maintain, obtain, sustain
ven/vent(来): prevent, invent, adventure, event
vert/vers(转): convert, reverse, diverse, version

### 状态类词根

cap/cept(拿取): capture, accept, concept, except
cede/ceed/cess(走): proceed, succeed, exceed, access
fac/fact/fect(做): factory, affect, effect, perfect
mit/miss(发送): submit, admit, dismiss, mission
pon/pos(放置): postpone, expose, compose, propose
sist/st(站立): insist, resist, consist, assist
struct(建造): structure, construct, destroy, instruct
tract(拉): attract, contract, distract, extract

### 感知类词根

audi(听): audience, audio, auditorium
sens/sent(感觉): sense, consent, sensitive, sentence
vid/vis(看): visible, vision, advise, evidence
voc/vok(喊叫): vocal, advocate, provoke, evoke

## 二、核心前缀

un-/in-/im-/dis-(否定): unhappy, impossible, disagree
re-(再次/回): return, review, rebuild
pre-(预先): preview, predict, prepare
pro-(向前/赞成): progress, promote, protect
ex-/e-(向外): export, exit, exclude
inter-(之间): international, interact, interview
trans-(跨越): transport, transfer, transform
sub-/suc-/sug-(在下面): subway, succeed, suggest
super-/sur-(超过): supermarket, surface, surpass
con-/com-/co-(共同): connect, combine, cooperate
de-(向下/去除): decrease, depart, delete
anti-/counter-(反对): anti-war, counter-attack
mis-(错误): misunderstand, mislead, mistake
over-(过度): overwork, overcome, overlook
under-(不足): underestimate, underground
en-/em-(使...): enable, encourage, empower
multi-(多): multiple, multimedia, multinational

## 三、核心后缀

名词: -tion/-sion, -ment, -ness, -ity/-ty, -ance/-ence, -er/-or/-ist, -ism, -ship
形容词: -ful, -less, -able/-ible, -ous/-ious, -ive, -al, -ic
动词: -ize/-ise, -fy/-ify, -ate, -en

## 四、四六级高频主题词

教育类: campus, curriculum, scholarship, diploma, academic, compulsory, optional, undergraduate, postgraduate, tuition, faculty, semester, graduate, major

科技类: technology, innovation, digital, artificial intelligence, virtual, device, software, hardware, network, data, cyber, breakthrough

环境类: environment, pollution, sustainable, emission, climate change, renewable, ecology, conservation, carbon, greenhouse, recycle

社会类: urbanization, globalization, population, employment, poverty, welfare, volunteer, charity, community, civilization, tradition

经济类: economy, inflation, investment, consumption, enterprise, commercial, financial, budget, trade, market, competition

健康类: physical, mental, nutrition, exercise, obesity, therapy, diagnose, symptom, prescription, epidemic, vaccine
