# 写作模板库与高级句型替换

## 晓艳写作哲学

模板是骨架，内容是血肉。先学会走，再学会跑。
不要怕用模板，但要学会灵活变通。

## 一、高分句型储备

### 开头句型

1. Recently, the issue of ... has aroused wide public concern.
2. It is universally acknowledged that ...
3. There is a growing concern about ...
4. With the rapid development of ..., ...
5. Nowadays, there is an increasing number of people who ...

### 观点表达

1. From my perspective, ...
2. As far as I am concerned, ...
3. I am firmly convinced that ...
4. There is no denying that ...
5. It goes without saying that ...

### 论证句型

1. First and foremost, ... Furthermore, ... Last but not least, ...
2. On the one hand, ... On the other hand, ...
3. There are several reasons accounting for this phenomenon.
4. A case in point is that ...
5. Take ... for example.

### 对比句型

1. Some people hold that ..., while others argue that ...
2. Compared with ..., ... has more advantages.
3. Contrary to popular belief, ...
4. Despite the fact that ..., ...

### 因果句型

1. The reason why ... is that ...
2. ... is attributed to ...
3. ... leads to / results in / contributes to ...
4. Owing to / Due to / On account of ...

### 结论句型

1. To sum up / In conclusion / All things considered, ...
2. It is high time that we put an emphasis on ...
3. Only in this way can we ...
4. Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely draw the conclusion that ...

## 三、连接词库

### 递进
moreover, furthermore, additionally, besides, what's more, in addition

### 转折
however, nevertheless, nonetheless, on the contrary, in contrast, whereas

### 因果
therefore, consequently, accordingly, hence, thus, as a result

### 举例
for instance, for example, such as, a case in point, to illustrate

### 总结
in conclusion, to sum up, in summary, all in all, in a word, on the whole

### 强调
indeed, in fact, as a matter of fact, undoubtedly, needless to say

## 四、书信写作模板

### 建议信
Dear [Name],

I am writing to offer some suggestions concerning ...

First and foremost, I would like to suggest that ... Moreover, it would be beneficial if ... Last but not least, I recommend that ...

I hope you will find these suggestions helpful. I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
[Name]

### 投诉信
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with ...

The reason for my complaint is that ... This has caused me considerable inconvenience.

I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter and take appropriate measures. I look forward to your prompt reply.

Yours faithfully,
[Name]

### 感谢信
Dear [Name],

I am writing to extend my sincere gratitude for ...

Without your help, I would not have been able to ... Once again, thank you for your kindness.

Yours sincerely,
[Name]

## 五、图表作文句式

### 描述图表
- As is vividly shown in the chart, ...
- The graph illustrates that ...
- According to the statistics, ...
- There was a significant increase from ... to ...
- The figure experienced a steady decline.

### 分析原因
- There are several factors accounting for this phenomenon.
- The rapid growth can be attributed to ...
- Among the various reasons, ... plays a vital role.
- This trend may be explained by the fact that ...

### 预测趋势
- Based on the analysis above, we can predict that this trend will continue.
- There is every reason to believe that ...
- If this trend persists, ...
